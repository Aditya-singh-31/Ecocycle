📋 How It Works

From Photo to Disposal Decision

📸 Image Capture

Users upload a photo of a waste item or drag it into the scanner tool.

🤖 AI Classification

EcoCycle AI analyzes shape, texture, and color to determine the waste category.

✅ Guidance Delivered

The platform recommends container type and safe disposal practices instantly.

📊 Impact Tracking

Collected data is used to measure recycling performance and inform sustainability actions.
